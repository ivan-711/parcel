## Summary
This is a fix and flip of a Cape Cod at 915 Erie Ave, Sheboygan, WI. The plan is a $139,000 purchase, a $38,000 rehab over 6 months, and a sale at $232,000. The numbers show a positive return, but the profit is thin for the work and time involved.

## Returns
Total profit over the hold is $12,126. The equity multiple is 1.34x, IRR is 56.5%, and annualized return on cash is 41.5%. Payback is 10 months. Net proceeds from the sale are $50,340 after selling costs of 8%. The percentage returns look strong only because the cash in the deal is small relative to the purchase. The dollar profit is modest.

## Financing
The loan covers 90% of cost at 11% interest with 2% in points. Cash needed at closing is $19,182. The most cash in the deal at once is $36,189, so the investor needs to fund well beyond closing as rehab draws and carrying costs come due. Interest runs through the 6-month rehab and the 4 months needed to sell, and any delay adds to that cost.

## Risks
- **Thin margin (caution flag).** Profit is under 10% of the resale price, so there is little room for surprises. A lower sale price, a rehab overrun, or a slower sale could erase much of the $12,126.
- **Exit price.** The whole deal rests on an after repair value of $232,000. The sale price at exit equals that figure with no cushion, so any miss on comps hits profit directly.
- **Timeline.** The plan assumes 6 months of rehab and 4 months to sell. Longer holds add interest, property tax of $2,800 per year, and insurance of $1,400 per year.
- **Selling costs.** At 8%, these take a large share of the gross gain, and the model leaves no room for price concessions on top.
- **Leverage.** High loan to cost and an 11% rate with points mean financing costs are significant against a small profit.

## Bottom line
The deal works on paper, but the margin is too narrow for comfort. I would want the after repair value confirmed with solid comparable sales and the rehab budget checked with contractor bids before committing. If either looks soft, the profit is at risk.
